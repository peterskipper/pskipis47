Captain Birthday is planning a party. All of his superhero friends are changing into their costumes to celebrate. Every hero ducked into a phone booth to change, but each came out with a little something extra. Before sending out the invites, Captain Birthday realized he had one last birthday superhero to add to the guest list. Who was it?

Stuck?

Hint 1 — how the rows work

Count the letters. Every jumble has one more letter than the word it's hiding. Unscramble the word into the blanks, and whatever letter is left over goes in the yellow box at the end of the row — that's the “something extra” the hero came out of the phone booth with.

Hint 2 — what to do with the extras

Eight rows, eight extra letters — but the final answer is longer than eight letters, so some get used more than once. The little emblems underneath the final answer boxes tell you which row each letter comes from: match the emblem to the badge at the start of the row.

What you're building is a superhero alias, two words, the sort of thing a newspaper would call a caped crime-fighter. And since it's a birthday party, there may be dessert involved.
